SHEET III · THE NINE FACTIONS · PORTRAIT · 63.5 × 88.9 MM
Reliquary28
Exhumation
RITE
Once per game each, play with any card: do not use its move or attack. Then discard this Rite.
Place a new Minion on any empty square in your back row.
Desecration
RITE
Once per game each, play with any card: do not use its move or attack. Then discard this Rite.
Remove a Ruin in your half. Place a new Minion on the cleared square.
Harrow
RITE
Once per game each, play with any card: do not use its move or attack. Then discard this Rite.
Take one slain enemy Minion from their pool; put your spare Minion on any empty square in your back row. It fights under your sigil.
Apocrypha
RITE
Once per game each, play with any card: do not use its move or attack. Then discard this Rite.
Swap one of your other two cards for any undealt deck card.
Advent
RITE
Once per game each, play with any card: do not use its move or attack. Then discard this Rite.
Each of your pieces may step 1 square, in any order.
Unction
RITE
Once per game each, play with any card: do not use its move or attack. Then discard this Rite.
Your Hero removes up to 2 damage tokens, at least 1.
Scourge
RITE
Once per game each, play with any card: do not use its move or attack. Then discard this Rite.
Deal 2 damage to an enemy within 2 of one of your pieces, in that piece's LoS.
Sanctuary
RITE
Once per game each, play with any card: do not use its move or attack. Then discard this Rite.
Your Hero cannot drop below 1 HP until the start of your next turn; the excess is never applied.
Anathema
RITE
Once per game each, play with any card: do not use its move or attack. Then discard this Rite.
Discard your opponent's Rite, unplayed, out of the game.

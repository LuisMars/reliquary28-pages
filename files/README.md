# Reliquary28: print and play

Everything here is laid out for **A4** paper, except
`reliquary-booklet-pages.pdf`, which is A5.

| File | What it is | Pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `reliquary-booklet.pdf` | the whole game, folded into an A5 booklet | 2 |
| `reliquary-booklet-pages.pdf` | the same rules, four separate A5 pages | 4 |
| `reliquary-cards.pdf` | 36 cards, nine to a sheet | 4 |

The card sheets are, in order: the nine agnostic moves, the nine unique moves,
the nine faction cards, and the nine Rites. Files with a language code in the
name, such as `reliquary-cards.es.pdf`, are the same documents in that language.

## The rules booklet

The rules are four A5 pages. Inside there is a setup diagram of a 9 by 9 board
and the first two turns of a card cycle, worked through card by card. Four
pages, and they are the whole game.

Those four pages come in two files. Same words, same diagrams, different
arrangement on the paper.

`reliquary-booklet.pdf` is the one to print and fold: one A4 sheet in
landscape, both sides, folded once down the middle. Its two sides carry the
pages in the order the fold needs, not the order you read them, so opening it
on screen looks scrambled. That is the file working, not the file broken.

**Print both sides, flip on the short edge, then fold down the middle with page
1 outward.** The flip edge is the one thing to get right here. Flip on the long
edge instead and every page after the first lands upside down and out of order,
which no amount of folding will fix.

`reliquary-booklet-pages.pdf` is the one to read: pages 1, 2, 3 and 4, each on
its own A5 page, in reading order. Use it on a screen or a tablet, or print it
as four A5 pages on a printer that will not do duplex. Nothing to fold, and
nothing to get wrong.

## PDF or browser?

The PDFs are the reliable route, and the one to use if you can. The card sheets
and the folding booklet are served as ordinary web pages too, at
`/print/cards/` and `/print/booklet/` (and `/es/print/...` in Spanish), so you
can print straight from a browser without downloading anything. The four A5
pages are a PDF only.

If you do print from the browser, set **Scale** to `100%` and **Margins** to
`None`, and turn off "Fit to page". Browsers fit to the page and add their own
margins by default, and that quietly rescales the sheet: the cards come out
slightly the wrong size, and you only find out after you have cut them. A PDF
cannot be rescaled by accident in the same way.

## Printing

1. **Print at 100% scale.** In the print dialog set *Scale* to `100%`; Adobe
   Reader calls this **Actual size**. Turn **off** "Fit to page", "Shrink
   oversized pages" and anything else that resizes the sheet.

   This is the one setting that matters. The cards are drawn at the standard
   **63.5 x 88.9 mm** poker size so they fit ordinary sleeves, and any scaling
   at all, even 97%, destroys that.

2. **Paper.** Give the card sheets the heaviest stock your printer will take:
   200 to 300 gsm is ideal, and ordinary paper glued to thin card works fine.
   The booklet is happy on plain 80 gsm, and it folds better on it.

3. **Single-sided, except the folding booklet.** The cards have no printed
   backs, so do not print duplex. `reliquary-booklet.pdf` is the one file that
   wants both sides of the sheet, flipped on the short edge, as above. If your
   printer will not do duplex at all, print `reliquary-booklet-pages.pdf`
   instead and you lose nothing but the fold.

4. **Black and white** is all you need. Every page is pure line work; there is
   no colour and no large areas of solid ink to drink your toner.

## Cutting

Each card sheet is a 3 x 3 grid with **crop marks in all four margins**. The
cards sit edge to edge, so every cut runs the full width or height of the page:

- Line your ruler or trimmer up with a matching pair of marks on opposite
  margins and cut straight across. Four cuts down, four across, per sheet.
- A guillotine or rotary trimmer gives far cleaner edges than scissors.
- Before you cut the rest, measure one finished card: it must be **63.5 mm wide
  and 88.9 mm tall**. If it is not, the printer scaled the page: fix the print
  settings and reprint rather than trimming the cards to fit.

## What else you need

The booklet has the full list. In short: a square grid board (8x8 up to
12x12), one Hero and three to five Minions per player, a Relic token each, two
six-sided dice, and damage tokens.

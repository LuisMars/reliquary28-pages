# Reliquary28

*Two warbands duel amid ruins for each other's holy relic.* Two players, about
20 minutes, on any grid you can draw.

**[reliquary28.luismars.com](https://reliquary28.luismars.com/)** &middot; free
print and play &middot; English and Castellano

## You hand your enemy every move you make

Three cards in your hand. Play one, set it aside, and take the card that was
waiting. The card you just played is the card your opponent picks up next turn.

Reliquary28 descends from Onitama and keeps its cruellest idea: the hand cycles
between you, so there are no secrets in it, only timing. Around that sits a
skirmish game. Two warbands, two relics standing in the open, ruins to break the
lines of sight, and a die counting down the peace.

## What you need

- A square grid, 8 by 8 up to 12 by 12. Chalk on a mat, a chessboard, graph
  paper, tape on a table.
- One Hero and three to five Minions each: any models you like, as long as the
  two sides can be told apart. Bigger boards take more bodies, or the armies
  never meet.
- Two relic tokens, two six-sided dice, and damage markers. One die is the
  Gamble die, the other counts the Vigil.
- The printed sheets, and something to cut with.

Players are not colours. You take a sigil: the Key or the Skull.

The card sheets are, in order: the nine agnostic moves, the nine unique moves,
the nine Faction cards, and the nine Rites. A file with a language code in the
name, such as `reliquary-cards.es.pdf`, is the same document in that language.

Print at 100%, actual size, with no fit to page: a browser that rescales the
sheet ruins the 63.5 by 88.9 mm card pitch. The booklet to fold wants both
sides, flipped on the short edge.

## How a turn goes

One card, one mode, then the card leaves your hand and walks across the table.
The four modes are MOVE, ATTACK, RALLY and RITE, and you take one of them, never
two.

Your Faction card gives you your attack and, for three of the nine factions, a
Passive. Your Rite is drafted at setup and fires once in the game.

## Winning

Slay the enemy Hero, or carry the enemy Relic home to your own back row. A
carrier drops the Relic when the other side pushes, pulls or swaps it, so a
stolen relic crosses the table hand to hand.

The Vigil die starts at 6 and falls by 1 every quiet round: no damage, no Relic
taken or dropped, no Rite. A quiet round at 1 ends the game, and the relics on
the board decide it.

## Two languages

English at `/`, Castellano at `/es/`. Both editions are the same game, cut from
the same sources, and the Spanish cards carry Spanish names: Peregrino, Saeta,
Garfio, Galerna.
